# Changelog

All notable changes to Barrel Jumper are listed here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and the project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.0.0](https://github.com/comprofix/barreljumper/commits/v1.0.0) - 2026-11-07

The first release: the Classic game, rebuilt with new artwork and sound.

### Added

- The four Classic screens: Barrels, Rivets, Elevators and Conveyor, in the
  original's order from level to level, with its movement, timing, hazards
  and scoring.
- The hammer, lost items, jump awards, the countdown bonus and extra lives at
  7,000, 50,000 and every 50,000 after that.
- One or two players taking turns, a starting level from 1 to 8, and Easy,
  Arcade or Hard skill for each player.
- High scores for each skill.
- Construction-site artwork: a worker climbing the girders, the thrower at
  the top and the captive to rescue.
- A site-board sidebar with the scores, the bonus, the level and the lives.
- An opening, a floor card before each screen, and an ending for each clear.
- Original sound effects, screen jingles and a title theme.
- A title screen with an attract demo.
- Keyboard and gamepad controls, fullscreen and scale modes.
- The game's own program icon.
